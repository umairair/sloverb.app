# 🎵 🔗 **[sloverb.app](https://sloverb.app)**   – Slow + Reverb / Nightcore for any song

## 🔥 Features  
✅ **Upload audio or use YouTube links**  

✅ **Real-time pitch & speed adjustment with a single control**  

✅ **Customizable reverb**  

✅ **Download your edited tracks** 🎶  

---

## 🎛️ How It Works  
1. **Upload an audio file** or **paste a YouTube link**  
2. **Adjust pitch, BPM, and reverb** in real time  
3. **Listen & tweak** to perfection  
4. **Download your masterpiece 🎵**
